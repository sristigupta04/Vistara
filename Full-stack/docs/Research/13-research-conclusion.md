## 2. What This Research Actually Established (Corrected)

1. Vistara's differentiation is evidence-backed: trust visibility is the one gap consistent across every competitor reviewed.
2. The 90-day plan is not "MVP now, ML someday" — it is a single sequenced delivery where the transactional platform (Phase 3–4) and the intelligence layer (Phase 5–8) both ship within 90 days, exactly as the project's own roadmap specifies.
3. AI/ML work is substantial and real: a recommendation baseline, a trust/risk classifier, review sentiment/aspect analysis, and photo/property verification — each with its own data, method, and evaluation step, not a single convenience feature.
4. What's genuinely deferred beyond 90 days (ID verification, business/certificate verification, experiences marketplace, Money Radar, regulatory compliance, trip planning, group travel, travel agency) is logged separately with clear reasoning, not vaguely labeled "Major."
