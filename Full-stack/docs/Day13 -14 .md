# 2. Final Problem Definition

The core problem identified for Vistara is not simply:

> "People need accommodation."

The deeper problem is:

```text
Users discover accommodation
        ↓
Information may be incomplete
        ↓
Trust is difficult to evaluate
        ↓
Reviews may not tell the complete story
        ↓
Requirements may be unclear
        ↓
Actual experience may differ
        ↓
Users need better decision support
```

Vistara therefore focuses on:

> **Trust-aware accommodation discovery and booking supported by verification, structured reviews, transparent requirements, AI-assisted discovery, and feedback mechanisms.**

# 10. ML Research Conclusion

