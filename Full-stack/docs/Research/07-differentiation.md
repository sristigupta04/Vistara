# Vistara — Day 7: Differentiation

> Confirms the Day 6 solution against competitors one final time before locking the USP. Reuses Day 2's competitor findings — no new research introduced.

## 1. What Vistara Will NOT Claim as Unique

Already common or emerging across competitors (Day 2):
- Search, filters, categories
- Reviews (as a concept)
- Booking, payment, wishlist
- AI-assisted search/recommendation

## 2. What Vistara Will Claim

Confirmed absent across every competitor reviewed in Day 2:
- Guest-visible property verification status (photo/listing-consistency checks) on the listing itself
- ML-based trust/risk scoring feeding into a visible trust status
- Reviews explicitly tied to verified completed stays, with AI-generated aspect summaries
- Host requirements confirmed and snapshotted at booking time
- A dedicated post-stay mismatch reporting flow feeding back into the risk model

## 3. Competitor vs Vistara (Trust-Specific)

| Capability | Airbnb | Booking.com | Agoda | Vrbo | Vistara |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Search/filters/booking | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Property verification shown to guest | No | No | No | No | **Yes** |
| Visible trust/risk status | No | No | No | No | **Yes** |
| Verified Stay review tag + review intelligence | No | No | No | No | **Yes** |
| Requirement snapshot | No | No | No | No | **Yes** |
| Mismatch reporting flow | No | No | No | No | **Yes** |

## 4. Final USP

**Short version:** *"Don't just find a stay. Know what you're booking."*

**Long version:** Vistara is a trust-aware accommodation marketplace that combines standard discovery and booking with visible ML-based property verification, a visible trust/risk status, verified-stay reviews with AI-generated summaries, confirmed and recorded host requirements, and structured post-stay feedback — none of which current major platforms surface to the guest.

## 5. Day 7 Conclusion

Differentiation is not a new feature — it's making an already-confirmed, universal gap (trust visibility) the product's center, instead of the backend afterthought every competitor treats it as.
